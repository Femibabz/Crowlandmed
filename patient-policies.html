Patient Policies

Important disclaimers, usage boundaries, emergency instructions, and care protocols.

Medical Emergency

Do not use this Website for medical emergencies. If you have a medical emergency, call a physician, qualified healthcare provider, or dial 911 immediately.

Under no circumstances should you attempt self-treatment or treatment of anyone else based on anything you have seen or read on this Website.

Not Medical Advice

The content provided on this Website is for informational purposes only. It is not professional medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or care, nor is it intended to be a substitute for professional care.

Always seek the advice of a qualified physician properly licensed to practice medicine or general healthcare in your jurisdiction concerning any questions you may have regarding any content obtained from this Website or any medical condition you believe may be relevant. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay in seeking it because of something you read on this Website.

No Physician-Patient Relationship

The presentation of content on the Website does not establish a physician-patient relationship between you (or anyone else) and the clinic owners or any of its physicians. It is not intended as a solicitation of individuals to become patients of the clinic.

No Endorsements

Unless specifically stated, Lundys Lane Medical Centre does not recommend or endorse any specific brand of products, services, procedures, business, or other information that appears or that may be advertised on this Website.

Out of Hours Support (Health811)

If you require medical assistance outside of our hours of operation, you may visit a local walk-in clinic, an emergency department, or call Health811 (formerly Telehealth Ontario).

Health811 is a free, confidential service you can call or access online to get health advice or information. A Registered Nurse will take your call 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
